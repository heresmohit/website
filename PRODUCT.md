# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML5, Vanilla CSS, lightweight static server (`serve` on Node.js), GitHub Actions deployment to GitHub Pages.

## Users

Primary: Event venues, festival organizers, cultural curators, and creative collaborators looking to book, co-produce, or host high-energy live shows and improv marathons.
Secondary: Founders and hiring leads evaluating candidates for high-impact Community Manager and Social Media Growth roles.

## Product Purpose

Serve as a fast, authentic, proof-driven portfolio that showcases Mohit's ability to drive real-world attendance, scale social reach, and produce sold-out live experiences (such as 12-hour marathons and international festival tours).

## Positioning

"Social Media & Live Event Producer": A proven operator with a pure focus on audience growth, high-converting viral reels, and packed rooms. Unlike theoretical marketers, Mohit manages end-to-end event execution—from on-ground front of house to multi-platform ticketing and digital community engagement.

## Operating Context

- Evaluated quickly by busy venue managers, festival directors, and community leaders.
- Key surfaces must show video proof (reels/shorts), attendee numbers, and direct contact options within seconds of landing.
- Needs seamless cross-device reading on mobile and desktop without layout shift or heavy framework overhead.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Zero AI Images / Data**: Strict constraint—no generated artwork, synthetic avatars, or fabricated case studies. All data, video embeds, and milestones must be 100% authentic.
- **Code-First Architecture**: Lightweight, handwritten semantic HTML and Vanilla CSS with zero external JS frameworks.
- **Embedded Real Media**: Native 9:16 responsive video embeds from `@improvlore` showcasing real crowd interactions and event clips.
- **Multi-Page Static Setup**: Fast loading pages with shared clean stylesheet and native dark/light mode.

## Brand Commitments

- **Tone**: Authentic, grounded, energetic yet understated (quiet luxury and editorial restraint over noisy neon gimmicks).
- **Identity**: Mohit Gumber / Improv Lore community producer.
- **Channels**: Improv Lore ([improvlore.com](https://improvlore.com), [@Improvlore](https://www.youtube.com/@Improvlore), [@improvlore](https://instagram.com/improvlore)), GitHub ([github.com/heresmohit](https://github.com/heresmohit)), Email (`hey@mohitgumber.com`).

## Evidence on Hand

- **180,000+ views** in 90 days across Improv Lore social channels (110k+ from non-followers), growing Instagram from 1,000 to 2,000+ followers.
- **Two 12-Hour Improv Marathons** produced with Underline Center (May 1 & Oct 2, 2026), selling 500+ tickets each.
- **100+ events produced** and 50+ front of house shows managed.
- **International festival tours**: Booked and toured shows in Singapore, Vietnam, Manila, and Hong Kong.
- **High-performing video assets**: Real YouTube Shorts (`kNfhCFrWWgY`, `n5pJw8PEJcE`, `G052V614sW0`, `9jCyzotY7KQ`, `OPQEeybb7Y0`).

## Product Principles

1. **Proof Over Prose**: Lead with real attendance metrics, actual video clips, and concrete ticketing outcomes.
2. **Speed and Simplicity**: Instant page loads, zero bloat, clean typography, and zero friction to contact.
3. **Restrained Craft**: Quiet, elegant presentation that lets the live performance and community energy take center stage.
4. **Creator-Ready**: Clear, straightforward HTML that can be updated in seconds without complex build toolchains.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Semantic HTML5 landmark hierarchy (`header`, `nav`, `main`, `section`, `article`, `footer`).
- High-contrast text supporting both dark and light modes via `prefers-color-scheme`.
- Keyboard accessible navigation and interactive form controls.
